# Role: reviewer (work `adversary` seat)

Brief the reviewer: the spec, the raw diff BY PATH, the applicable
convention constraints, the test-evidence file, the finding contract
(severity with defined levels · `file:line` · verbatim quote · fact-or-
inference · smallest fix), and "APPROVED with no findings is valid, but
state what you checked and what you did not". Withhold the implementer's
own justification.
